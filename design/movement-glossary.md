# `movement` — Spanish glossary for the qigong pages

2026-10-15. Status: **approved by David 2026-10-15** (design/movement.md,
step 5; his answers at the end). Once approved, every Spanish set page (step 6) follows it, so
the 33 pages read as one collection. Iberian Spanish, as on the other
sites.

## Style conventions

- **Instructions use the informal imperative** ("Levanta los brazos",
  "Respira hondo"), matching the site's "Respira. Muévete. Practica."
  and the "tú" of the other sites. (The alternative, common in Spanish
  exercise sheets, is the infinitive: "Levantar los brazos". See
  question 1.)
- **Chinese terms stay in pinyin, in italics, with a Spanish article and
  gender**: *el qi*, *el dantian* (inferior, medio, superior), *el
  wuji*, *el taiji*, *el yin* y *el yang*, *el bagua*, *el mingmen*, *el
  santishi*, *el jiangan*. Accents as in the English pages (*qì*,
  *Bā Duàn Jǐn*). Chinese characters stay as they are.
- **Repetitions stay as "(x3)"**; "3 times" becomes "3 veces".
- **Left/right:** izquierda/derecha; "L, R" is written out.
- **Quotation marks:** «comillas angulares», as in music's Spanish
  ("usa el enlace «Abrir»").
- **Book and article titles stay in their original language**, in
  italics. Names of teachers, schools and websites are unchanged.
- **Acupressure point codes stay as they are** (K27, B23, GB-21, LI-11,
  GV 4).
