**Getting ready for summer: overcoming pain, stiffness and fatigue.**
(A nice reference: *Meridian Exercise for Self-Healing*, by Ilchi Lee.)

## Warm up

- **Earth:** tap, massage from toes to head.
- **Metal:** loosen up the joints; say hello to the bones.
- **Water:** ring the temple bell; flow up/down, left/right.
- **Wood:** swirl *qi* from heaven to earth; dragon breaths.
- **Fire:** snap the *yin*; expand into the cosmos.

## Overcome fatigue

- **Inner thigh stretch:** squatting, one knee bent, the other straight.
- **Open leg meridians:** sit, pound sides of legs with a gentle fist.
- **Sole clapping:** sitting, clap soles of feet together.
- **Bicycle exercise** (advanced): push legs up, bicycle motion.
    - (Alternative: standing or sitting hip stretches.)
- Pull *qi* from the heavens, lift *qi* from the earth.
- **Push hands to sky and earth:** cross hands, turn to bow stance, step
  forward to warrior pose with back right foot on toes, forward left
  knee bent; reach up and look at left hand, push down with right; repeat
  with right foot forward.

## Relieve aches and pains

### Lower back

- **Leg sweep:** horse stance, bend forward, massage back of legs.
- **Side-to-side knee twist:** lying on back, arms wide.
- **Wag your feet:** table pose; cat and cow; lift feet left and right.

### Neck

- Neck pulls and turns (also good for the shoulder).
- **Move the jar:** horse stance, form a triangle with palms facing down;
  push the "jar" down to left and right; at center, bring knees together,
  arms at side, push down.

### Shoulder

- **Pushing palms:** wide stance, prayer position; push.
- **Arm rotations:** warrior pose with back foot on toes; push hand over
  forward knee forward; push hand over back foot up in a circular
  rotation; switch sides.

### Sciatica

- Iron hand pushes the sky.
- Butterfly bend.
- **Extend leg to toe touch:** sitting, one leg tucked under; reach up,
  hands together, reach forward towards extended toe; switch. Advanced:
  extend leg to side and reach sideways.

### Osteoporosis

- **Body vibration:** slightly wide stance, palms facing at belly level;
  relax, start shaking at hands and arms, then extend to every cell in
  the body.
- **Swing arms, limbs dropping:** lift right arm, bending knees, while
  swinging left arm back; pause, continue.
