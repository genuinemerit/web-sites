## 24 Section Brocade Qigong

### Kneeling or sitting

- Up and Down Lower Abdominal Breathing, 24 times.
- Embrace Ribs, Move Shoulders, Breathe Deeply, 20 times.
- Hands Behind Neck, Open/Close Arms, 20 times.
- Hands at Base of Spine, Breathe Deeply, 20 times.
- Expand Chest, Pull Arms at Neck Left/Right, 10 times each.
- Head to Shoulder and Vibrate, 12 times each side.
- Head to Chest and Back, 12 times each front/back.
- Turn Head Left/Right, Vibrate, 12 times each side.
- Tilt Head, Knock Ear 6 times, Upper/Lower Jaw 6 times each.
- Tap at Top of Spine with Left/Right Little Finger, 6 times each.
- Face Upward, Tap Forehead with Left/Right Fist, 6 times each.
- Massage the Eyes, 2 times.

### Feet and legs together in front

- Whole body bend and grasp toes, 10 times.
- Stretch up with arms and bend back, 20 times.

### Standing

- Waterfall arm rotations to front, side, back, 20 times.
- Single-arm fist full circles to back, 10 times each arm.
- Bend knees, at waist, drop neck, grab toes; lift, 10 times.

### Stances

- Forward one step, other foot at an angle, arms on hips, face angle,
  turn, bend back, return; left/right, 6 times each.
- Diagonal horse stance, feet wide at 80 degrees, raise hands with fist,
  20 to 30 times.
- Hands on waist, split legs to the utmost, 2 times.

### Standing

- Legs apart, palms on knees, sink forward, head up, bending back, 20
  times.
- Fly raised arms, head turns, right/left, 10 times each.

### Feet and legs together in front

- Stretch forward, roll back, arms to floor, hold body up using head and
  shoulders, 10 times.

## Pushing and Pulling the Qi (Daoyin-tu)

**Snapping Yin.** From *wuji* posture, raise right palm and left leg.
Release stale essence through hand and foot. Pull fresh *qì* into the
lower elixir field (*dantian*), below navel, above perineum. Repeat with
left palm, right leg.

**Slowly Extend Limbs.** Lift arms slowly, palms down, wrists relaxed.
Lift left leg at knee, then slowly straighten it. Balance while drawing
in fresh energy. Repeat lifting right leg.

**Gather Qi from Front and Back.** Circle arms from behind, palms down,
leaning forward to embrace a ball of *qi* with slightly bent knees,
slightly arched back. Turn palms up, lower the ball of *qi* to the lower
elixir field while straightening back and knees.

**Slanted Flying.** Embrace a ball of *qi* at abdomen, right palm facing
left palm. Extend right hand 45 degrees up, left 45 degrees down, to
sides. Return to embrace with hands reversed. Extend opposite.

**Dragon Ascending.** Arms wide akimbo, palms down. Reach down,
squatting, arms crossed. Lift up to chest level, collecting. Reach up
wide, palms up. Gather back to chest level. Open, reach down, repeating.

**Sowing Seeds.** Close stance, hands nurturing *qi* at abdomen. Swing
left, as if throwing seeds, while right hand reaches gently down, back
to other side. Repeat with right hand sowing seeds.

**Turtle Move.** Feet shoulder-width or wider. Arms out at shoulder
height. Right hand on top, carry a small ball of *qi* to right as far as
comfortable. Rotate, placing left hand on top, turning to the left.
Meditate while moving slowly, placing the heart-mind focus on the lower
elixir field.
