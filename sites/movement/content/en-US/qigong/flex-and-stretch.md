## Quick joint warm-up

- Ankle circles
- Knee circles
- Hip circles
- Shoulder circles
- Neck circles

## Dancer's warm-up stretches

- Shoulder flex, "patty-cake"
- Shoulder girdle, cross arms in front
- Spinal twist
- Trunk flex, bend side to side, arms at side to side
- Trunk rotate, palms together, twist side to side
- Hip flex, lifting out
- Hip flex, lifting in
- Hip flex, stepping back
- Hip flex, three-point stepping
- Knee, hamstring stretch, one foot slightly forward, toe touch
- Plantar flex, on toes, toes down, sides of feet

## Taiji warm-up

- Reach for the heavens… and turn… and tilt to side… and reach forward
  to toes
- …and squat, and rock
- Horse stance with slight pigeon toe (*wing chun* stance):
    - Right hand on abdomen, left hand behind, rotate at abdomen.
    - Right hand on abdomen, left hand above, thumb at solar plexus, flow
      up and down to heart.
    - …same, but with one foot forward.
    - Right hand on abdomen, left hand above, at chest, flow up and down
      to shoulders.
    - Flow up and down to shoulders, into both arms reaching forward,
      palms down.
    - …same, into one arm, with the other arm behind the back.
    - Both arms forward, rotate at wrists down, up, around, using full
      flow.
    - One arm forward, one behind back, palm facing in, rotate "break a
      grip" using full flow.
    - Coil both arms forward and back, starting with hands in front of
      chest, full flow.
    - One hand on abdomen, same foot slightly forward, coil other hand
      forward and back.
    - Settling, pushing the wrists; first both hands, then one hand.
    - Rotate the ball, held close to abdomen but with full rotation.
    - Push *qi* to the sides, draw back in.
