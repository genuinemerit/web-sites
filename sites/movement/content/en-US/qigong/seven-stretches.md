1. **Spine Rotation:** Broomstick Twist, seated.
2. **Spine Extension:** Cross-Body Stretch; bow stance, twist towards
   forward knee; switch.
3. **Spine Flexion:** Ankle Grab.
4. **Lateral Flexion:** Side Body Stretch; one leg long, the other in
   front of it, twist in each direction; switch.
5. **Shoulders:** Reverse Shoulder Stretch; seated, knees bent, arms
   back, hands down, fingers pointing out, elbows directly above hands;
   move forward until the stretch is felt in the shoulder.
6. **Hips:** Single-Leg Frog; hands on hips, one leg back on knee and
   toes, other leg lateral, straight knee, on heel, like a partial
   split; switch.
7. **Balance:** Walk a Line; or Wave Hands Like Clouds; or Seven Stars
   posture; etc.
