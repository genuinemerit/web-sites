**Qigong exercises to shake the kinks out.**

## Cleanse, Find and Clear Blocked Energy

- *Wuji* stance. Relax analytical and emotional mind. Let wisdom mind
  take over.
- Pull *qi* down from the heavens.
- Lightly tap on the head with fingertips.
- Tap with 4 flat fingers at top of spine.
- Ring the temple bell, slapping the "Letting Go" and "Elegant Mansion"
  points.
- Tap arms with fist: outside, fingers to back; inside, armpit to
  fingers.
- Tap legs with palm or side of hand: outside, glutes to feet; inside,
  feet to pelvis.
- Massage palms.
- Expand chest: hands over groin, raise forward, cross, look left, look
  right.
- Spread wings: hands behind, palms out; circle around to navel, up,
  push, lift, return.
- Massage abdomen.
- Ring the temple bell, tapping the lower back, "Sea of Vitality".
- Massage palms.
- Rub face.
- Comb the hair: run fingers from front to back on scalp.
- Nurture *qi* at the "Sea of Energy" (core, lower *dantian* / energy
  field).

## Tone and Energize

- Wise owl gazes backwards.
- Bow and arrow.
- Raise single iron arm.
- Hip circles with palms on "Sea of Vitality".
- Horse stance: turn trunk and push palm (*taiji* punch and pull).
- Press palms on feet.
- Bow stance, squat and stretch.

## Cultivate the Qi

**Breath work**: either standing or sitting, no particular pose
required.

- 10-20 deep abdominal breaths, without strain, bringing attention to
  breath at nostrils.
- While continuing deep, slow, thin, comfortable, ever-quieter
  breathing:
    - Bring the spirit home: focus attention starting with top of head,
      down to toes.
    - Open and close the sense gates: for each sense, reach outwards,
      then inwards.
    - Smile inwardly: send gratitude to heart, lungs, kidneys, liver,
      spleen.
    - Feel energy arrive, absorb via belly, top of head, perineum, soles
      of feet, palms, knees, elbows, shoulders and hips, via pores;
      expanding and contracting around you.
    - Visualize breath condensing into bone marrow, strengthening immune
      systems. Expanding through muscles, tendons, skin and hair,
      warming and healing.
    - With eyes open, using palms, lift natural-born "original" *qi* up
      from belly (lower "water/*yin*" elixir field) to heart (middle
      "fire/*yang*" elixir field), mixing with "ingested" *qi* to augment
      reserves, settling back down to lower field. Allow, but don't
      force, any excess *yin* energy to continue up to top of
      head/forehead (upper "spirit *qi*" elixir field), mixing with
      "original" (universal) *qi* to lift spirit.
    - Circulation of *qi* and deep breathing begins to happen naturally,
      without intention. Just let it happen. Do it without doing
      anything.
    - Return to normal breathing.

## Train the Qi

- Belly rolls forward and back, while rocking on heels and toes.
- "Full Martha Graham" flow, left and right, then to diagonals, from the
  core.
- Reach for the sky, then push up to the sky from front, from back.
- New qigong therapy stepping; toes, heels, soles of feet, with long
  breaths.
- Seven stars concentration, then free-form seven stars standing, on
  right foot, on left foot.
- Bird frolic.
- Bounce the *qi*: springy feeling in legs, elbows in, feeling energy
  flows.
- Step forward: *taiji* push up, from shoulders and hips.
- Three body forces standing: feel the 3 powers: springing forward,
  turning inward, lowering pelvis and lifting head.
- Pushing and stepping: release all *yang* energy into a hand while
  taking a step forward; prepare the other hand/foot, filling them with
  *yin* energy to be transformed in the next step.
- Always keep a cool, calm mind.
- Nurture *qi* at core.
