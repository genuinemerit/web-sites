Start with 70% **abdominal breathing**: front of belly, to sides
(lateral breath), lower back, upper back. Avoid moving upper chest.
Relax. Slow.

*Do all movements slowly and gracefully. Avoid excessive effort. Use
wave-like motions.*

## Opening

In Buddhist prayer position, raise hands to forehead, then do *taiji*
opening, then step to left to *wuji* position, then push down, relax
fingers, rotating palms inward, down (x3), then standing post, eyes open,
focused on palms.

## Presenting the Pestle 1, 2, 3

- Pull arms in to open prayer position, separate, push forward, then:
- Push the horizon; look forward and down.
- Lift arms circularly to side of head, relax.
- Push up to the heavens, tuck in buttocks, raise heels, looking
  straight or down, not up.
- Repeat 3x.

## Plucking Stars on Each Side

- Turn waist, swinging arms around.
- Press left arm back on *mingmen*.
- Right arm stretches up to pluck a star,
    - then stretches it down along the center line.
- Lower heels to ground.
- Smooth circular motion, keeping shoulders level, not one higher than
  the other.
- Eyes follow moving hand.
- Focus mind on waist and kidneys.
- Repeat 3x.

## Pulling Nine Cows by Their Tails

- Cross arms, then slowly move into
- deep diagonal bow, left foot and hand back,
- one hand back, the other forward.
- Both hands close finger by finger, to a fist.
- With fists, pull/close, big reach/extend, both hands, turning spine
  and waist without stepping.
- Open hands at full extent when reaching, close fists when pulling.
- Move body weight forward and back 3x.
- Eyes follow the forward fist.
- Repeat 1x each side.

## Showing Talons and Spreading Wings

- Raise arms to side, then circle to front.
- Relax shoulders.
- Forward push, palms forward, spreading fingers in tiger claw, bending
  forward from waist, exhale.
- Focus on heels of palms, heels of feet; eyes slowly begin to glare.
- Withdraw to armpits or above head, around sides in a circle, inhale;
  eyes relax; stretching chest.
- Forward: as if opening a window, then crushing a mountain.
- Pulling: as if retreating with the tide.
- Repeat 3x.

## Nine Ghosts Drawing Sabers

- Palms tucked in near shoulders.
- Turn whole body right, *qi* ball, then open, circling both hands
  across,
- then curving arms in different directions.
- One arm on back of head, touching ear, covering back of head.
- Other arm on back, pushing up as far as possible.
- Open elbows when bending up, close elbows when squatting down.
- Turn head when bending.
- Keep looking up.
- Big bends down and up 3x.
- Twisting up to side with arm behind head; down to side with arm at
  back,
- with slightly bent knees, but maintain a slight horse stance with
  knees forward.
- Big windmill to change direction.
- End by unwinding arms, pushing to horizon.
- Reverse, 1x in each direction.

## Sinking the Three Bodily Zones

- Very wide stance, arms wide, toes forward.
- Palms rotating up, then pressing down.
- Exhale saying "hai".
- Don't lift too high, work on lowering the squat.
- Lift arms wide as if lifting a heavy weight.
- Mind focused on full squat, actual squat according to mobility.
- Squat more deeply each time (3x), focus on the 3 energy reservoirs.

## Black Dragon Displays Its Claws

- Very wide stance.
- Arms wide, or one arm reaching out, closing fingers one by one into a
  fist, lower both fists.
- Reach right, then across torso with tiger claw or flat/*vajra*
  posture, palm up.
- Then withdraw palms, reach down to toes, knees straight, reach across.
- Back up the other side, making a fist when pulling up.
- Other arm remains a fist at middle, by waist.
- Keep waist straight while turning the body.
- Eyes follow moving hand and palm.
- Repeat each direction 3x.

## Tiger Springing on Its Prey

- Start with a mild "wing chun" stance.
- Turn left foot in more.
- Step forward with right foot to a very wide stance.
- Similar to the cat frolic, with arms grabbing/rotating.
- Then squat all the way down onto one knee and/or long back-leg
  stretch.
- Push claws (fingers) to floor if possible, or to knees according to
  mobility.
- Eyes glare, spinal and leg stretches 3x.
- Repeat each direction 1x.

## Bowing Down in Salutation

- Turn toes inward, then step out slightly with right foot to balanced
  stance.
- Reach palms over ears, behind head.
- Lower head first, then cervical spine, then thoracic spine, then
  lumbar spine.
- Tap nape of the neck 7 times.
- Stretch forward 3x or 1x, deeper each time, according to mobility.
- Keep knees straight, body and mind relaxed.
- If hypertension, do not lower the head.
- Sharp release of arms to side when done with the last bend.

## Swinging the Tail

- Press on ears and then release suddenly.
- Push forward, with palms pressed together, then
- hands interlocked, turn palms out or palms pressed, stretch forward.
- Pull elbows in, then stretch down to ground.
- Keep knees straight; reach down as far as mobility allows.
- Then look side to side, tucking in buttocks, as if looking back at
  buttocks, raising the head.
- 3x each direction.
- At end, lift up in a big arching movement, arms above, and lower them
  slowly to abdomen into the closing form.

## Closing form

- Lower arms, thumbs tracing the conception vessel (along front of
  body).
- Pause at the abdomen.
- Push palms down gently into *wuji* posture.
- Repeat 3x.
- Withdraw left foot to closed stance.
