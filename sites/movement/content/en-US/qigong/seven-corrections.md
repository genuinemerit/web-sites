**Rotate.** Move from the waist. Think big circular motions, not just
waving hands around.

**Concentrate.** Eyes follow the main flow of hands. Spirit is expressed
from the eyes.

**Solid.** Touch the ground in a solid way. Power arises from legs and
core.

**Flexible.** Strive for curves. Soft, gentle movements, not rigid.

**Coordinated.** Everything is connected. Move arms and legs in sync.

**No excess, no deficiency.** Not too much, not too little. Not too lax,
not too stiff.

**Intention.** Gather, tone, energize. Prepare to move. Let it flow.
Touch the power point, the furthest extent of a stretch or movement,
then recover back to the starting point.
