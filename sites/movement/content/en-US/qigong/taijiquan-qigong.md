**Taiji qigong, after Dr. Yang, Jwing-Ming.**

## Warm-up

### Standing

- Lift *qi* to heavens, palms up, hold 10 s.
- Turn left, turn right, hold 5 s.
- Tilt left, tilt right, hold 5 s.
- Bend forward, touch ground, do not round back, hold 5 s.
- Squat, feet flat. Then raise heels 10 times.

### Horse stance

- Hands on belly, sacrum. Do hip circles.
- Hands together. Push down from abdomen to perineum.
    - Repeat while turning slowly side to side, 10 times.
- Lift hands from abdomen up to chest.
    - Increase length to shoulders, moving shoulders back, 10 times.
- Extend arms forward, palms down.
    - Push one hand forward, the other behind back with a slight twist, 5
      each side.
- Hold hands forward, palms down.
    - Rotate hands down, 10 times. Rotate hands outward, 10 times.
    - One hand forward, one behind back, rotate wrist in one direction,
      10 times.
    - Rotate wrist in the other direction, 10 times.
- Coil arms in to chest, elbows out. Coil arms forward, palms up,
  exhaling. Coil in, palms down.
    - One hand coiling out while the other coils in, gathering, at
      abdomen, 10 times.
- Arms out to side, then round in to *taiji* push.
    - One hand push, with the other hand at abdomen.
- Vigorously rotate the ball of *qi* while turning left and right, 10
  times.
- Push and gather *qi* from the horizons.

### Breathing / *Nei Dan*

- *Taiji* opening with Daoist breathing.
- Embracing the Moon.
    - One foot forward, toe touching only, weight mainly on back foot.
    - Tongue to roof of the mouth.
    - Arms embrace a circle at or above the chest with fingers almost
      touching.
    - Calm, relax, breathe deeply. Focus attention on shoulders.
    - Dave's extensions:
        - Three deep breaths in, piled up; three relaxed breaths out.
        - Breathe in for a count of five; breathe out for a count of
          five.
        - Five elements meditation: Earth, Metal, Water, Wood, Fire.
