**Qìgōng (life-energy cultivation) references.**

## Books

**Dr. Yáng, Jwìng-Mīng, *The Root of Chinese Qìgōng: Secrets for Health,
Longevity and Enlightenment*, YMAA Publication Center.** See
<https://www.ymaa.com> and <https://ymaainternational.com/>

Dr. Yáng is the founder of Yang's Martial Arts Association, which
started in Jamaica Plain (Boston) and now has dojos internationally.
He's written many books on tai chi, kung fu, qigong, massage and
meditation. This book covers: history, basic concepts and categories of
qigong; theories of *qi*, essence, spirit and the human body, including
channels and vessels; points for improving practice and regulating body,
breath and mind.

**Ilchi Lee, *Meridian Exercise for Self-Healing: Classified by Common
Symptoms* and *Water Up, Fire Down*, Best Life Media.** See
<https://www.bestlifemedia.com> and <https://ilchi.com/>

Ilchi Lee is a meditation instructor who founded the Earth Citizen
Movement and developed a form of practice called Brain Education that
incorporates aspects of yoga, tai chi and Korean Sundo (Daoist
meditation). His *Meridian Exercise* book covers many stretching/yoga-type
exercises aimed at relieving specific symptoms of unease, pain or
illness. The *Water Up, Fire Down* book focuses on energy circulation.

**Michael Davies, *JIANGAN: The Chinese Health Wand*, Singing Dragon.**
See <https://uk.singingdragon.com/products/jiangan-the-chinese-health-wand> and
<https://youtu.be/11VRNrKEa2A?list=PLR2J2RAlH-Q4icVYbR-7cO1PEVTNTmhHm>

Michael Davies is a *taijiquan* instructor who popularized a practice
of muscle-toning movements for health and vitality using a wooden
exercise wand that purportedly has ancient origins. He renamed this set
*Taiji Jiangan*, *taiji* referring to its flowing movements and
*jiangan* a way of saying "exercise wand" in Mandarin. This form was
originally described in Bruce L. Johnson's 1977 book *Chinese Exercise
Wand*.
