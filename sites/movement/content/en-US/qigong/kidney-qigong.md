**Kidney-related qigong / *qi* meridian exercises.**

## Circulation Exercise

- Lie on back. Extend legs, arms, raise head. Shimmy and shake for 1-2
  minutes. Repeat 5 times.

## Upper Body Lift

- Lie on stomach with palms on floor under shoulders. Push up, arching
  the back. Hold, while focusing attention on spine. Exhale. Repeat 3
  times.

## Sitting Forward Bend

- Sitting with legs straight. Rotate both arms 360° and reach to toes 10
  times. On the last reach, hold bottom of feet and bend forward as much
  as possible, exhaling. Relax. Do the bend-and-hold 3 more times.

## Lift Legs Over Head

- Lie on back, arms extended, palms down. Inhale, raise legs over head,
  extending as far as possible, hold a few seconds, exhale and return.
  Repeat 3 times.

## Raising Hands

- Standing stance. Left foot forward. Reach right arm back, shifting
  weight, and focus attention on the horizon with the moving hand,
  stimulating lower abdomen and waist. Same with right foot / left hand.
  Repeat 2 times for each side.

## Face-Down Toe Touch

- Lie on stomach, arms akimbo. Inhaling, reach left leg over to right
  hand. Focus on waist, hold a few seconds. Exhale, release. Same with
  right leg / left hand. Repeat 2 times for each side.

## Sole Patting

- Tap 30 times, then massage the sole of the foot with thumb or a
  massage stick for 5 minutes. Try to find/focus on the *yong quan*
  acupressure point (Ki-01, "the bubbling spring") just behind the ball
  of the foot, about 1/3 of the distance from toes to heel, in the
  center of the foot near the base of the big toe.

## Kidney (*shen shu* and *zhi shi*) Massage

- Standing. Place thumbs on the *shen shu* ("bladder 23") points, on
  each side of the second lumbar vertebra. Inhale, hold breath and press
  with thumbs, gently tilting upper body backwards. Release, exhale.
  Repeat two times.
- Then do the same at the *zhi shi* ("bladder 52") points, on each side
  of the second lumbar vertebra, about 2 cm outside the *shen shu*
  points.

## Kidney Stimulation

- Standing, feet close together, hands in prayer position raised to
  mouth level. Inhale, rotate hands left with fingers pointing away until
  left elbow is behind. Keep facing forward. Bend knees and upper body
  with focus on kidneys. Exhale. Repeat on left side.

## Back Leg Lift

- Lie on stomach, hands at sides with palms on floor. Inhale, lift left
  leg, keeping chin on floor. Exhale and lower gently. Inhale and do the
  same with right leg. Repeat the set 2 more times.
- Inhale and raise both legs, with focus on lower abdomen and kidneys.
  Gently lower legs.

## Direct Kidney Massage

- Sitting on floor. Rub hands to activate *qi*. Press on kidneys,
  fingers pointing down, and massage in an up-and-down motion.

## Ankle Acupressure

- Sitting, pull left, then right foot forward and massage the Ki 3, 4,
  5, 6 and 12 points with thumb and index finger ("great ravine", "large
  goblet", "water spring", "shining sea", "great manifestation").

## Abdominal Breathing

- On exhale, focus on sending energy to the GV 4 *ming men* point,
  located just below the 2nd lumbar vertebra.
