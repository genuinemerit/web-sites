title: "Taiji for Balance"
---

**Taiji for Balance** is a simplified taiji form intended to help folks
improve their balance and thus prevent falls. Based on parts of the
[Guang Ping Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guang_Ping_Yang_t'ai_chi_ch'uan)
style, it was developed by
[Lucy Bartimole](https://www.youtube.com/@shiftwithlucy).

In these videos, the instructor performs the movements twice (a "double
set"), in both the traditional direction and the "mirrored" direction.

In the front view, the mirrored direction is first, followed by the
traditional direction. In the rear view, the traditional direction is
first, followed by the mirrored direction. The intention is to help
students following the video imitate the instructor's movements. There
is no sound on these videos.
